# TestLab Code of Conduct

## A welcoming learning community

TestLab welcomes people of every experience level and background. Everyone participating in project
issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces is expected to help make them respectful and
safe, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or level of technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat questions and mistakes as opportunities to learn. Explain patiently and avoid belittling people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect different experiences, opinions, identities, and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Take responsibility for harmful behavior and cooperate with moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation,
sustained disruption, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are not acceptable.
Neither is publishing private information without permission or continuing unwanted contact.

These expectations also apply when someone represents TestLab in another community or public space.

## Reporting a concern

The repository owner, [@aalampatil](https://github.com/aalampatil), is responsible for moderation.
Use an available private contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. If none is available,
open an issue titled "Code of conduct contact request" asking for a private reporting channel. Do not
include names, incident details, or personal information in that public request. There is currently no
dedicated private conduct-reporting address published by this project.

You can also use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for abuse on GitHub, especially if your concern
involves the repository owner. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers will consider the context, impact, and any repeated behavior. They may request a correction,
issue a warning, remove or edit harmful content, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently ban
a participant. Severe misconduct may result in an immediate ban.

Reports should be handled discreetly, sharing information only as needed to review and address a
concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Maintainers must apply these expectations fairly
and must not retaliate against people who raise concerns in good faith. Anyone seeking reconsideration
may request it through the same private channel with relevant context.
